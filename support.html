Frequently Asked Questions

How do I backup my diary entries? +

Go to Settings > Offline Backup & Export. Tap Export Full Journal (JSON) to save a complete portable backup, or tap View as Printable Text to copy your diary as readable Markdown.

What happens if I forget my 4-digit PIN? +

On the PIN lock screen, tap Forgot PIN?. You will be prompted to answer the security question you selected during setup (e.g. your childhood best friend or favorite book). If verified, you can reset your PIN immediately without losing your journal entries.

Can anyone see my diary or does it upload to the cloud? +

No. My Diary has no cloud server, no user accounts, and zero internet tracking. All entries stay strictly inside your physical phone's encrypted SQLite database.

Is my journal data completely private? +

Yes. My Diary has no cloud server, no user tracking, and operates 100% offline. All entries, photos, and settings remain exclusively on your phone's storage.

How do I transfer my diary to a new phone? +

1. On your old phone, go to Settings and tap Export Full Journal (JSON).
2. Save or send the JSON text file to your new phone.
3. Install My Diary on the new phone, go to Settings, tap Restore from JSON Backup, and paste your JSON backup payload.
